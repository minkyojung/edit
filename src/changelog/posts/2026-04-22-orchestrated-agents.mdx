Today we're opening the private beta for **orchestrated agents** in Octave.
You can now spin up multiple AI agents in parallel — each working on its own
slice of the project — and merge their work back without losing context.

## What's new

- Parallel agent runs with shared workspace state
- Per-agent task queues, surfaced in the sidebar
- One-click rollback of any agent's changes

## Why it matters

Single-agent tools force you to wait. With orchestrated agents, planning,
implementation, and review can happen at the same time — and you stay in
control of what merges.

william@octave.dev.
